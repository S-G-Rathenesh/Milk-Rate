Milk Rate Mobile App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 7, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Milk Rate ("we," "our," or "the application"). Milk Rate is a mobile dairy delivery record-keeping and billing utility built for Android. The application serves two distinct user roles:

  • Milk Sellers / Vendors: Dairy business owners and independent milk distributors who manage customer lists, log daily morning and evening deliveries, set unit milk rates per litre, and generate monthly financial summaries.
  • Customers / Consumers: Milk recipients who view their own daily delivery records, monthly consumption volume, and calculated bills.

We respect your privacy and are committed to transparent data practices. This Privacy Policy details what information the application accesses, how it is handled, where it is stored, and your choices regarding privacy.

2. Information Collected from Customers & Sellers

Milk Rate collects only the minimal operational data required for dairy accounting:

A. Milk Sellers / Vendors

  • Authentication Information: Mobile phone number and security PIN used to authenticate seller access, or Google Account profile information (display name, email address) when using Google sign-in.
  • Business Identity: Dairy or business display name (e.g., "Sri Krishna Dairy") presented on customer receipts and reports.
  • Price Settings: Standard milk price per litre (in INR) and timestamped rate change history.

B. Customers / Consumers

  • Customer Profile (Managed by Seller): Customer display name, delivery phone number, and optional delivery address entered by the vendor to identify the delivery account.
  • Zero Password / Zero Login Requirement: Customers do not create passwords or submit SMS OTPs. Customer access is established by claiming a unique invitation QR code or link generated by their milk vendor.
  • Anonymous Device Identifier: Upon claiming an invitation, an anonymous guest identifier (Firebase Anonymous UID) is bound to the customer's device to securely authenticate read-only access to their delivery records.
Sensitive Data We DO NOT Collect: Milk Rate does not collect, process, or store bank account numbers, credit or debit card details, UPI PINs, government identity numbers, camera rolls, biometric records, or real-time GPS locations.

3. Account Information & Phone Number Handling

Phone numbers are utilized solely for account identification and delivery routing. Seller phone numbers authenticate seller dashboard access. Customer phone numbers link deliveries to customer profiles. We do not sell, rent, or trade phone numbers to third-party telemarketers or data brokers.

4. Milk Delivery Records, Quantities & Billing

The core function of Milk Rate is tracking physical milk delivery quantities and billing calculations:

  • Daily Delivery Entries: Calendar date, delivery session (Morning or Evening), exact volume delivered in millilitres (e.g., 500 ml, 1000 ml), delivery status (delivered or zero / skipped), and the historical milk rate applicable on that date.
  • Financial Calculations: Total monthly volume (litres) and computed cost based on the applicable rate per litre.
  • Historical Integrity: Rate adjustments apply strictly to deliveries on or after their effective date. Past delivery entries and previously generated monthly bills remain immutable.

5. Local Storage & Database Handling

Milk Rate is architected with an offline-first design:

  • Local SQLite Database (expo-sqlite): All customer records, daily delivery entries, rate histories, and offline queues are stored locally on the device in an SQLite database. The application operates fully even without an active internet connection.
  • Local Preference Storage (AsyncStorage): User interface preferences, including selected theme (Light, Dark, System), language choice (English or Tamil), and cached consent states are stored locally on the device.

6. Internet Connectivity & Data Synchronization

When an internet connection is available, the application communicates securely over HTTPS / TLS with cloud backends:

  • Cloud Synchronization (Cloud Firestore): When cloud sync is enabled, delivery records and customer invitations synchronize with Google Cloud Firestore servers to allow customers to view their records remotely.
  • Single-Use Cryptographic Invitations: Customer invitation links use 128-bit cryptographically secure random tokens. Tokens are validated atomically on the server, can only be redeemed once, expire after 7 days, and can be revoked by the seller at any time.

Customer Only

8. Google Mobile Ads SDK & Advertising Identifiers

The customer application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (provided by Google LLC). Depending on your Android OS version, device settings, and privacy consent:

  • The SDK may access your device's Google Advertising ID (AAID) to serve advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset advertising ID.
  • When non-personalized ads are served or consent is withheld, ad requests do not use the advertising identifier for ad personalization.
  • Banners automatically unmount when customer tabs are not focused to minimize device resource and network utilization.

9. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) & Consent

Milk Rate integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to support privacy regulations (including GDPR, UK GDPR, and relevant regional privacy frameworks):

  • Explicit Consent Gathering: Users in applicable jurisdictions are presented with Google's certified consent dialog prior to receiving personalized advertisements.
  • Ad Request Gate: Advertisements are requested only when permissible under the user's consent status (canRequestAds = true).
  • Revoking or Updating Consent: Customers can update their advertising privacy preferences at any time by navigating to Profile > Ad Privacy & Consent > Ad Privacy Settings within the customer app.

11. Data Retention & Account Deactivation

Milk Rate enforces strict business rules regarding data retention:

  • Customer Deactivation: When a milk seller deactivates a customer account, the customer's login access is revoked. However, historical milk entries, monthly bills, and financial calculations are never deleted or altered. Historical records remain permanently available for vendor reports and tax/accounting integrity.
  • Reactivation: Reactivating a customer restores customer access without modifying or losing any historical delivery data.
  • Permanent Deletion Requests: Users desiring complete deletion of personal records may submit a written request to the publisher contact details below. Validated deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

12. Children's Privacy

Milk Rate is a commercial record-keeping tool intended for dairy vendors and adult household bill payers. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

13. Data Security

We implement multi-layered safeguards to protect your records:

  • Encrypted Transport: All network communications with cloud servers utilize HTTPS with TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Cryptographic Random Tokens: Invitation links use 128-bit hardware-entropy tokens generated using cryptographically secure random number generators.
  • Server-Side Rules: Cloud Firestore security rules prevent unauthorized access, ensuring customers can read only their own assigned delivery records and cannot create, modify, or delete entries.
  • Local Isolation: Local SQLite databases are sandboxed within the application's private Android storage directory.

14. User Privacy Rights & Choices

You maintain full control over your privacy choices:

  • Access & Review: Customers can inspect their entire delivery history and monthly bills directly in the application.
  • Ad Consent Choices: Customers can modify or revoke their advertisement consent choices at any time via Profile > Ad Privacy Settings.
  • Advertising ID Reset: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising Identifier in your device's system settings.
  • Offline Usage: Sellers can operate the app entirely offline without creating cloud accounts.
