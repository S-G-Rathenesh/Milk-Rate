Milk Rate – Google Play Compliance

Account Deletion & Data Removal Request

Application: Milk Rate for Android  |  Last Updated: November 8, 2026

Google Play User Data Transparency Notice:

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, this page provides clear instructions on how users of the Milk Rate mobile application (package com.milkrate.app) can request the permanent deletion of their account credentials and personal identifying information.

1. Overview of Account Structure

Milk Rate serves two distinct types of users:

  • Milk Sellers (Vendors): Vendors who sign in via Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) to maintain customer registers, daily milk quantities, pricing rules, and monthly billing totals.
  • Customers (Households): Customers who access their daily milk delivery receipts by scanning a secure QR code invitation provided by their local milk vendor, without needing a separate login password.

2. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Because Milk Rate protects commercial delivery records against accidental deletion, the application does not provide an automated self-service account wipe button inside the client UI.

All account deletion requests are reviewed and processed manually by the developer administration within 30 calendar days.

A. For Milk Sellers (Vendors)

To request permanent deletion of your vendor account:

  1. Send an email to the developer at ratheneshsg@gmail.com from the Google Account associated with your seller profile.
  2. Include the following information in your request:
    • Registered Email: Your Google Sign-In email address
    • Dairy / Business Name: The business display name configured in the app
    • Registered Phone Number: Contact number linked to your profile
    • Confirmation: A clear statement confirming you wish to permanently delete your seller profile and cloud database record.
  3. Once identity is validated, your Firebase Authentication identity, cloud Firestore seller document (/sellers/{uid}), and active invitation links will be permanently deleted.

B. For Customers

If you are a customer receiving milk delivery updates:

  • Option 1 (Direct with Vendor): You can contact your milk vendor directly and ask them to remove your profile from their customer directory. This immediately disconnects your profile and revokes all active QR access tokens.
  • Option 2 (Central Developer Request): Send an email to ratheneshsg@gmail.com specifying your name, mobile phone number, and the name of your milk vendor. The developer will coordinate with the vendor record to permanently scrub your personal data.
Important Clarification: Customer Deactivation vs. Permanent Deletion

When a milk vendor marks a customer as "Deactivated" inside the seller app, customer login access and advertising are suspended, but previous transaction logs remain intact for billing reconciliation. Deactivation is not permanent deletion. If you desire complete data erasure, an explicit deletion request must be submitted as described above.

3. Data Deletion & Retention Schedule

To maintain financial integrity and comply with accounting guidelines in India, certain numerical audit data must be retained:

Data Category Action on Deletion Request Retention Period & Justification
Account Credentials
(Google OAuth UID, Firebase Auth token)		 Permanently deleted Immediate removal upon processing (within 30 days).
Personal Profile Data
(Seller Name, Dairy Name, Phone, Email, Customer Names)		 Permanently deleted Purged from cloud database (Firestore) and cache within 30 days.
Invitation Codes & QR Tokens Permanently revoked & deleted Invalidated immediately; prevents any future access.
Historical Delivery & Billing Ledger
(Delivery dates, sessions, quantities, rates, settlement status)		 Anonymized & Retained on vendor ledger Retained as numeric accounting records for up to 7 years in accordance with commercial bookkeeping and tax/audit requirements. All personal identifying names and phone numbers are stripped or replaced with anonymized placeholders (e.g., [Deleted Customer]).
